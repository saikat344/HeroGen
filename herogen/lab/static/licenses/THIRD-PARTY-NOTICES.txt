https://lucide.dev/license

The Lucide icons in herogen/lab/static/icons/ (every file not named hg-*) are modified from lucide-static
1.64.0: drawn at stroke width 1.5 instead of 2, and given a <title>, role="img" and an aria-label naming
what the Lab uses them for. The page itself drops the title, role and label again when it inlines an icon.
