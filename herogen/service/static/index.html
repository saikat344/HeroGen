How it works

From a line of text to a rigged hero.

Every step is saved as it lands, so you watch your character take shape instead of waiting on a spinner.

  1. 01Sketch

    Your words become a full-body pencil sketch, or bring your own drawing.

  2. 02Artwork

    Coloured character art, then front, side and back views.

  3. 033D shape

    The views become a mesh you can orbit, in clay or wireframe.

  4. 04Textured hero

    A repainted texture and a GLB file and, when every Roblox check passes, an R15 body.
